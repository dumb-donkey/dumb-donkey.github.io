Sidekick privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. Sidekick is made by Dumb Donkey (Zeno).

The short version

Sidekick has no server. It does not collect, send, sell or share anything about you or your tickets. Everything it shows and everything you type stays in your own browser, on your own computer.

What Sidekick reads

Only on the Freshservice address you allow (you approve it once in your browser's own permission prompt), Sidekick reads the ticket list and, when you open one, that ticket's details and conversation. It never changes, closes or replies to anything.

That address is the one you type or paste in the setup screen: either yourcompany.freshservice.com, or the address your company's help desk uses when you sign in. Sidekick asks your browser for permission for that single address only. It never asks for, and cannot read, any other website, and nothing is read from the address until you approve it.

Managers: Sidekick has a hidden Manager mode (Settings, About). In it you pick the techs on your team inside Sidekick. To show the list of techs to pick from, Sidekick reads Freshservice's list of agents and groups and keeps only each person's name, number and group, never an email address or phone number. For the techs you pick it reads their open tickets (kept on your computer, like your own) and their resolved and closed tickets, of which only ticket numbers, dates and counts are kept, never titles or requester names. When you open a list or a ticket, its titles and conversation are read from Freshservice for that moment and are shown on your screen only; they are never saved. This uses the same Freshservice login and the same rules as everything else: Sidekick only shows what your login can already see, and it never changes anything.

What is saved, and where

Ticket titles, requester names, dates and statuses; email addresses it looks up so you can write to a requester; your private notes, reminders, Library entries, settings, and screenshots you save. All of this is saved only in your browser's extension storage on your computer. The text of ticket conversations is shown on screen and is not saved. Removing the extension removes all of it.

What leaves your computer

Nothing about you or your tickets. Two optional extras contact the outside, and only if you turn them on: weather sends only the city you typed to open-meteo.com (or wttr.in as a backup); news downloads the public headline feed of the publisher you pick. Teams, Outlook and Freshservice open in your browser tabs only when you press a button for them.

Backups

If you make a backup, it is a file saved to your own Downloads folder by your own click. You may protect it with a password. Sidekick never uploads it.

Children

Sidekick is a work tool for help-desk staff and is not directed at children.
